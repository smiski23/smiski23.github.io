本アプリ「百鬼夜行札」は、氏名・メールアドレス・位置情報・端末の識別子など、個人を特定できる情報を一切集めません。

本アプリは、ダウンロード時に購入いただく買い切りの有料アプリです。追加のアプリ内課金はありません。広告、アクセス解析、外部サービスへの送信も行いません。第三者に情報を提供することもありません。購入の手続きと決済は Apple の App Store が行い、当方が利用者の支払い情報や個人情報を受け取ることはありません。

4. 通信について

本アプリはオフラインで動作し、ゲーム中にインターネット通信を行いません。

5. お子さまの利用

個人情報を集めないため、年齢にかかわらず安心してお使いいただけます。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The app "Hyakki Yakou Fuda" (百鬼夜行札) does not collect any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, location, or device identifiers.

2. Data Stored

Your play records (such as your rank, unlocked charms and companions, and your settings) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer's servers. Deleting the app also deletes these records.

3. In-App Purchases, Advertising, Analytics, and Third Parties

This is a paid app purchased once at download. There are no additional in-app purchases. The app does not display advertising, does not use analytics, and does not send data to any external service. We do not provide any information to third parties. Purchase processing and payment are handled by the App Store; we never receive your payment information or personal data.

4. Network Communication

The app works fully offline and does not communicate over the network during gameplay.

5. Children

Because the app does not collect personal information, it can be used safely by people of any age.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it through an app update and on this page.
